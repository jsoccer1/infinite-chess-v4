    How to Play

    Watch the video walkthrough (opens in a new tab)

    Infinite Chess is chess on a figure-8 board of 72 cells. Each of the four lanes (Q, B, N, R) is a loop that never ends: rooks, bishops and queens keep sliding around the board until something blocks them.

    • White starts in the bottom loop and Black in the top loop (left and right in the horizontal layout). White moves first.
    • The 16 squares in the middle are where the two loops cross. Lanes pass through each other there.
    • Pieces move like normal chess pieces along the board’s lanes and diagonals. Knights jump two steps and one sideways.
    • Bishops, queens and kings can cut diagonally across the narrow tip of each center hole (R7 to R1, and Q12 to Q18). Pawns and knights cannot.
    • Once a pawn enters the 16-square center diamond it can never step back into the loop it came from.
    • Pawns move toward the other loop and promote when White reaches a cell numbered 15 or Black reaches a cell numbered 4.
    • Checkmate wins. Stalemate and threefold repetition are draws.
    • Turn on “Show cell names” in a game to see every cell’s name.
